# Overview

The **Main Screen** is composed of:
- The **"2D Grid Keyboard"** as the centerpiece for playing melodies.
- The **"Control Panel"** to control accompaniment playback and real-time recording.

Key Features:
- **Play across a vast range with the "2D Grid Keyboard"**
  - Spanning 8 full octaves arranged in a 2D grid, allowing instant access to high and low registers simply by scrolling.
  - No need to repeatedly press octave up/down buttons; play intuitively with multi-touch gestures.
- **Real-time Jam Session with Multi-layer Accompaniment**
  - Toggle Tracks (T), Chord Accompaniment (C), Drum Patterns (D), and Metronome (M) on and off with single taps to practice solos or jam with backing tracks.
- **One-tap Improvisation & Recording (TrackJam)**
  - Simply play the keyboard while listening to the backing tracks to record your live performance in real time. Undo (↺) or Clear mistakes with a single touch.

# Playback Controls

Control playback, stopping, and toggling of individual accompaniment layers.

![play](../assets/images/main/main_play.jpg)

| Button | Normal Tap (Action) | Long-Press Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`Play` / `Stop`** | Start / Stop playback of all backing layers | Show playback start position dialog (Select Bar Group/Sub) <br> ![](../assets/images/main/main_playback_position.jpg)|
| **`T:ON` / `T:OFF`** | Toggle Track playback (Tracks 1〜3 + TrackJam) ON/OFF | Show individual mute dialog for each track (Tracks 1〜4)<br> <img src="../assets/images/main/main_track_onoff.jpg" width="300">|
| **`C:ON` / `C:OFF`** | Toggle Chord accompaniment playback ON/OFF | - |
| **`D:ON` / `D:OFF`** | Toggle Drum sequencer playback ON/OFF | - |
| **`M:ON` / `M:OFF`** | Toggle Metronome ON/OFF | - |

The left section displays the currently playing bar position and the corresponding chord name set in the Chord screen.

# Recommended Workflow & Jamming Guide

## 1. Play Simply as a Keyboard Instrument
1. Tap the **Inst** button at the top to choose your desired instrument preset (Piano, Electric Piano, Strings, etc.).
2. Turn ON the **Lock** switch to enjoy smooth glissandos and agile keyboard runs without accidental scrolling.

## 2. Jam Along with Accompaniment & Record in Real Time
1. Turn ON **C:ON** (Chords) and **D:ON** (Drums) on the bottom panel.
2. Tap the **Record** button at the top.
3. After a 1-bar count-in, the backing tracks start automatically. Play your melody on the keyboard in sync with the rhythm.
4. When finished, tap **Save**. Listen back to your recorded phrase anytime with **T:ON**.
5. If you make a mistake, tap **↺ (Undo)** to instantly retry.
6. Recorded phrases are saved into the Jam Track and can be further refined on the Track screen's piano roll editor (including quantizing loose timing).

## 3. Practice or Record from a Specific Bar
1. Long-press the **Play** or **Record** button to specify a starting bar position (e.g., Bar Group or Sub-bar).
2. Focus on practicing difficult sections or re-recording specific phrases effortlessly.

# Pro Tips & Techniques

> [!TIP]  
> **Make Use of Long-Press Shortcuts**
> - **Long-press `Record`**: Open punch-in recording dialog from a specific bar (perfect for re-recording a specific phrase!).
> - **Long-press `Play`**: Open playback start position dialog (ideal for looping and practicing a hook or bridge!).
> - **Long-press `T:ON`**: Toggle solo/mute for individual tracks (Tracks 1〜3 and TrackJam).

> [!NOTE]  
> **Switching between Scroll and Glissando Modes**
> - **Scroll Mode (`Lock: OFF`)**: Drag on screen to freely navigate to different pitch registers.
> - **Glissando Mode (`Lock: ON`)**: Locks screen scrolling so you can slide your finger across keys for fast glissando runs and accurate chord playing.
> **Turn `Rotate` ON**
> - Vertically aligns the keys, increasing the number of visible horizontal keys for a natural, wider playing range.

> [!IMPORTANT]  
> **Punch-in Recording Behavior**
> When starting recording from a specific bar (e.g., Group 2 - Sub 1) via long-press `Record`, **only TrackJam notes from that point forward are overwritten**. Notes prior to the starting point are safely preserved.

> [!TIP]  
> **Rotate Screen for Different Play Styles**
> Turning ON the **Rotate** switch rotates the keyboard 90 degrees. This makes two-thumb playing while holding your smartphone vertically or fast one-handed scale navigation remarkably comfortable.
