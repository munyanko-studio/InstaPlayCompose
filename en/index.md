# Overview

**InstaPlayCompose** is a lightweight music creation and jamming app for smartphones that combines intuitive touch keyboard performance with versatile multi-track accompaniment (chords, drums, and melody tracks). Compose and jam anytime, anywhere right from your phone.

You can share your musical projects or export them to **MIDI, MP3, and OGG** formats.

As a simple use case, you can improvise on the responsive on-screen keyboard, save your performance, and play it back instantly.
You can also edit drum patterns and sequences, configure chord sequences, and fine-tune notes in the piano roll editor.

From left to right:

- [Main Screen](main)
  - Composed of the expressive "2D Grid Keyboard" and the "Control Panel" for playback and recording.
  - Play and record keyboard melodies in real time alongside chord and drum accompaniments.
- [Track Screen](track)
  - Edit notes visually using the piano roll editor.
  - Supports 3 standard melody tracks plus a dedicated Jam Track (holding notes played/recorded on the Main screen).
- [Chord Screen](chord)
  - Assign chord types and accompaniment styles bar by bar.
- [Drum Screen](drum)
  - Select preset or custom drum patterns and arrange them across bars.

# ⚠️ Limitations

- Only 4/4 time signature is currently supported.
- Supports up to 256 bars (up to 396 bars for the Jam Track).
